About the Author

Leena is a 22-year-old psychology graduate and fiction writer with a particular interest in stories about power, control, and the systems people live within.

She wrote the first drafts of both Government Property and Godless during her final year of university, somehow balancing a psychology degree with the apparently much more sensible decision to invent entire fictional societies at the same time.

Her stories often explore wider structures such as religion, bureaucracy, hierarchy, authority, and the ways individuals adapt to—or resist—the systems built around them.

She is especially drawn to complicated worlds where power is not always obviously good or evil, and where people may have very different reasons for participating in the same system.

Writing is the fun part.

Editing, unfortunately, is not.

Nevertheless, with multiple completed drafts and far too many future book ideas, she intends to stick with it.
