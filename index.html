About the Author

I’m Leena, a 22-year-old psychology graduate and fiction writer with a particular interest in stories about power, control, and the systems people live within.

I wrote the first drafts of both Government Property and GODLESS during my final year of university, apparently deciding that completing a psychology degree was not enough work on its own.

A lot of my writing explores wider social structures such as religion, bureaucracy, hierarchy and authority, especially the ways people adapt to, benefit from, resist, or become trapped within them.

I’m drawn to worlds where power is complicated rather than obviously good or evil, and where the system itself can be just as important as the characters living inside it.

Writing is the part I love.

Editing is the part I have unfortunately discovered I hate.

Still, I have finished drafts, more books planned, and far too many ideas to stop now.
