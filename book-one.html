Government Property

Everyone knows what happens when the government recalls you.

You go. You train. You serve.

And you don’t get to leave.

For twenty years, Arica has barely stepped beyond her own garden. Sheltered, trained and prepared for a world she has never actually experienced, she sees her recall as freedom.

Eamon knows better.

His family tried to hide him. He turned himself in anyway.

Now they’re both inside a government facility where careers are assigned, obedience is expected, and service lasts until retirement.

At first, their biggest problems are each other: Arica is painfully literal, frighteningly capable and completely unprepared for normal human interaction. Eamon is sarcastic, privileged, perpetually hungry and determined to treat the entire institution like one enormous inconvenience.

Then the training begins.

The tests become crueler. The rules become harder to question. And the line between preparing people to serve the government and teaching them to survive it begins to disappear.

Somewhere between blood, bureaucracy and badly timed jokes, Arica and Eamon become something neither of them expected.

But they were never brought there to choose their own futures.

They were brought there to become Government Property.
