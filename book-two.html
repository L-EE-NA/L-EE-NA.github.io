GODLESS

Her kingdom had no gods. Its conquerors have plenty.

Princess Vera has lost everything.

Her parents are dead. Her kingdom has been burned to the ground. Its survivors have been dragged beneath the earth and worked in chains by the very people who destroyed them.

Vera is supposed to accept that she is no longer a princess.

She has no intention of doing so.

With Valerion—the last surviving captain of her royal guard—and a stolen uniform, Vera escapes her cell and walks straight into the ranks of the enemy. What begins as a desperate attempt to survive becomes something far more dangerous: an infiltration, a growing rebellion, and a chance to take back what was stolen from them.

But the kingdom that conquered hers does not see its victory as theft.

It sees it as divine right.

Here, blood can sanctify conquest. Ritual can turn brutality into law. Kings answer to covenants older than themselves, and behind them stand powers Vera neither understands nor believes in.

To reclaim her people, Vera will have to survive a world built around gods she refuses to worship.

And soon, taking back her kingdom may be the least dangerous thing she has to do.

After all, what can the gods do with a girl who doesn’t believe in them?
